AI-Assisted Consultation Preparation

MediConnect features an integrated AI Assistant engineered specifically for pre-consultation organization. It structures patient concerns into clear bullet points for clinicians without attempting autonomous medical diagnosis.

Symptom Organization

Categorizes reported symptoms, timeline, and triggers into a structured briefing for the clinician.

Empowering Clinical Questions

Generates relevant questions patients can ask during their visit to ensure productive consultations.

Document Summarization

Summarizes complex health report texts into plain-language key points for discussion.

Strict Ethical Guardrails: The AI Assistant will never diagnose illnesses, prescribe pharmaceuticals, recommend drug dosages, or substitute professional clinical evaluation.
